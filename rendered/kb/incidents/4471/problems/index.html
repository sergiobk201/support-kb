The problems

parser: the shared parser read every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month came out swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3 told day-first tenants to type the month first. Its date field printed MM/DD/YYYY. People typed what it said, and the form read the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix did not touch this path. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the path is gone.

one-day: invites and reminders show the day before. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The date keeps its month and loses a day, which no day/month swap can do; it looks like a time zone shift. The desk read these reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) but files them under parser by tenant. The parser fix cannot move a date by one day, and the engineering update does not mention them. Nothing in the kb explains them yet.

#4471 closed with the parser fix, and the hint problem ends with the booking-v3 form. The one-day reports have no fix and no explanation. If another one comes in, reopen its ticket; do not merge it into parser.
