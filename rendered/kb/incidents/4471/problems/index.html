The problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month come out swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” This is the fix #4471 has queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what it says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. The parser fix does not touch this path, so these reports keep coming after #4471 closes.

one-day: invites and reminders show the day before. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The date keeps its month and loses a day, which no day/month swap can do; it looks like a time zone shift. The desk read these reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The cause table on #4471 still files them under parser by tenant.

The parser fix closes the parser ticket only. Keep the hint and one-day tickets open until their own fixes ship.
